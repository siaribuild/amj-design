## Referral program — the detail

The basics are on the [referral program page](/refer) — what you get, what your mate gets, and what has
to happen before we pay. This page covers the questions that come up afterwards.

### Getting credit for a referral

**What if my mate already has an account with us?**

Then there's no referral. The program pays for introductions to businesses that aren't ours yet, so a
referral is only recorded when a genuinely new account is created. If someone already has an account —
even a dormant one they made years ago and forgot about — following your link won't record anything.

**What if two of us refer the same tradie?**

The first referral recorded is the one that counts, permanently. If someone follows your link and a
different code is given when their account is set up, the second one is refused rather than replacing
yours. It works the same way in reverse, so it's worth sharing your code early rather than assuming.

**I told someone about you months ago and they're only ordering now. Do I get anything?**

Only if a referral was recorded at the time — meaning they used your link or your code when they
created their account. We can't add one retrospectively. This isn't us being difficult: once an
account exists and has been quoted, there's no honest way to establish who introduced them.

**Can I refer my own other business?**

No. That includes a second company you're a director of, a trust you're a beneficiary of, or another
business under the same ownership or control. It also includes making a second account under a
different email. The program is for introducing other people's businesses.

**Do they have to use the link, or is the code enough?**

Either, but the code has to be given when their account is set up — not added afterwards. The link
does that automatically. Otherwise they just tell us the code when we're setting them up, so reading
it out over the phone or on site works perfectly well; that's what it's for. What can't happen is
adding a code to an account that already exists.

### Getting paid

**Why do you need my ABN and bank details before I even have a code?**

Because a reward we can't pay isn't a reward. If you earn money and we have nowhere to send it, that
money sits with us — and money owed to someone but unpaid for a long time stops being a simple IOU and
becomes a legal obligation with its own rules. Asking up front means that never happens.

It also means that when you do earn something, there's nothing to chase. It's already set up.

**When exactly do I get paid?**

When your mate's first order is **paid in full** — not when they order, not when they pay a deposit,
not when it's delivered. After that, within the payment window on the program page.

We pay on full payment because it's the point where the job is genuinely done. It means you're never
in the position of being paid and then asked for it back because something fell through.

**How do I know when something's happened?**

Your account's Referrals section is the live picture: who you've referred, how far along each one is,
what's confirmed, and the date each payment is due. We email you when we've actually paid you, with
the reference you'll see on your statement.

Everything else is on the screen rather than in your inbox — deliberately. A referral moves through
several stages that need nothing from you, and an email for each one is noise you'd learn to ignore.

**What if my mate's order is cancelled or refunded?**

If it happens before we've paid you, nothing is payable. Once we've paid, it's yours — we don't ask
for referral rewards back.

**What counts towards the minimum order?**

The goods, excluding GST and excluding delivery. So an order that reaches the minimum on the invoice
total might not reach it on the figure we measure.

**Why is my percentage on the goods and not the invoice total?**

Two reasons, and both are about not paying you a share of money that was never ours. GST isn't our
revenue — we collect it and pass it to the ATO. Delivery is a cost we pass on rather than something we
make anything on. Paying a percentage of either would mean paying you out of money we don't have.

**Can I change my bank details?**

Any time, from your account. The only moment we won't let you clear them is when you've got a
confirmed reward we haven't paid yet — those are the details we're about to pay into.

If you do clear your details, your code stops working until you put them back. Anything you'd already
earned is safe.

**Is it taxable? What about GST?**

The amount we quote is what lands in your account, GST included if you're registered. Your own tax
position is between you and your accountant — we're not able to advise on it.

### Time limits and changes

**How long does my mate have to place their first order?**

The qualifying period is on the program page, measured from when the referral was recorded. If they
order after it, the referral has lapsed and there's nothing to pay. Their discount runs out at the same
time.

You can see the deadline for each of your referrals in your account, so it's never a surprise.

**What if the rate changes after I've referred someone?**

Your referral keeps the terms it was recorded on. If we raise the rate, referrals recorded before the
change stay at the old one; if we lower it, the same. Whatever was true on the day it was recorded is
what we pay.

**What if you close the program?**

Referrals already recorded run their full period on the terms they were given, rewards you've already
earned still get paid, and a discount already given to someone you referred still applies to their
first order. Closing the program stops new referrals being recorded — it doesn't cancel anything
already promised.

**Can I leave?**

Yes — removing your payout details takes you out of the program. Anything you've already earned is
still paid.
