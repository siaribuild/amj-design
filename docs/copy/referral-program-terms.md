## Referral program terms

These terms apply to the AMJ Trade Direct referral program. By joining the program you accept them.

The rates, minimum order value, time limits and payment window that apply to your referrals are the
ones shown on the **referral program page** at the time each referral is recorded. If we change them
later, your existing referrals keep the terms they were given.

### 1. Who can take part

You can join if you have a registered AMJ Trade Direct account in good standing, and you have given us
an ABN and the bank account details we would pay you into.

**You do not need to have bought anything from us to refer someone.** Joining costs nothing and
requires no purchase, ever.

You cannot join if your account is one of our own staff accounts.

A person you refer counts as a referred tradie only if they are genuinely new to us — no existing
account, no previous order, and not the same business as you under a different login.

### 2. How we know who referred whom

There are two ways a referral gets recorded, and only two:

- Someone follows your referral link and creates an account.
- Someone types your referral code into their own account after signing in, before they place their
  first order.

Both work the same way, and both depend on the other person identifying themselves. **There is no way
for you to give us someone else's name, phone number or email address**, and we will not accept those
details if offered.

Some other rules that decide who gets credit:

- A referral is recorded only when a **new account is created**. If someone already has an account
  with us, following your link does nothing — they are not a new customer, whoever introduced them.
- Following a link records a referral for a limited period after the click. That period is on the
  program page.
- **One referral per referred account, permanently.** The first one recorded is the one that counts,
  and a second code cannot replace it.
- **There are no chains.** You earn on the tradies you refer. You do not earn on anyone they go on to
  refer.

### 3. What has to happen before you are paid

All of these:

1. The tradie you referred places their **first order** with us. Later orders do not earn anything —
   the reward is for the introduction.
2. That order is at least the **minimum order value** shown on the program page. The minimum is
   measured on the goods excluding GST and excluding delivery.
3. That order is placed within the **qualifying period** shown on the program page, measured from when
   the referral was recorded. After that the referral lapses and pays nothing.
4. The order is **paid in full**. Not deposit paid, not delivered — paid in full.

If the order is cancelled or refunded before we pay you, nothing is payable.

### 4. What you get paid

Your reward is a percentage of the referred tradie's first order. The percentage is on the program
page.

The percentage is calculated on the **goods only, excluding GST and excluding delivery**. It is not a
percentage of the invoice total. We work it out this way because GST is collected on the ATO's behalf
rather than being ours, and delivery is a cost we pass on rather than something we earn on.

If a maximum reward per referral applies, it is stated on the program page.

**The figures that apply are the ones in force when your referral was recorded.** If we change the
rate afterwards, referrals already recorded are unaffected — in either direction.

### 5. What the tradie you refer gets

They get a discount on their first order. The discount is on the program page.

It applies to their first order only, it runs out when the referral's qualifying period ends, and it
is already included in the prices they see — there is nothing for them to apply or redeem.

### 6. How and when we pay you

We pay by bank transfer to the account on your program details, within the payment window shown on the
program page, measured from the day the referred order is paid in full.

You need a valid ABN and complete bank details on your account before you can join, and they must stay
current for us to pay you. If you remove them, we hold any further reward until you put them back.

We do not pay by cheque, credit against your account, vouchers, or points.

### 7. Changing or ending the program

> **FLAGGED FOR LEGAL REVIEW.** A right to vary or end an agreement "at any time in our absolute
> discretion" sits on the unfair-contract-terms grey list (ACL s 25(b) and (d)), and for a
> standard-form small business contract the burden is on us to show such a term is reasonably
> necessary. This clause is drafted narrowly and with notice for that reason. A lawyer should confirm
> the drafting and the notice period, and set the actual number of days.

We may change the rates, minimum order value, qualifying period or payment window for **future**
referrals. We will publish the change on the program page before it takes effect.

We may close the program to new referrals. If we do, the program page will say so.

**Whatever we change, and whenever we close the program:**

- Referrals already recorded run out their full qualifying period on the terms they were given.
- Rewards you have already earned are still paid, on the timetable in clause 6.
- A discount already given to a tradie you referred still applies to their first order, for as long as
  it was going to.

We may suspend or end your participation if you breach these terms, or while we review a referral
under clause 8. If we do, we will tell you why.

### 8. Fraud and abuse

The program is for genuine introductions to businesses that are not already ours. These do not count,
and we will not pay on them:

- Referring yourself, including through a second account or a second email address.
- Referring another business you own or control, or that is under common ownership or control with
  yours — for example a company you are a director of, or a trust you are a beneficiary of.
- A referral where the referred account is not genuinely a new customer.
- Creating accounts on someone else's behalf, or entering a code for them.

Where we think one of these applies, we will tell you, put the referral on hold while we look at it,
and give you a chance to explain before we make a decision. **We will not withhold a reward without
telling you why.**

Deliberate abuse can also mean we close your participation in the program.

### 9. What you and the tradie you refer each get to see

**You will see**, for each person you refer: their business name, how far along they are (signed up,
ordered, paid), and what you have earned. You will not see what they bought, what their project is,
or their contact details.

**They will be told**, when they use your code, that the person who referred them is paid a percentage
of their first order once it is paid.

### 10. Your privacy, and theirs

To pay you we collect and hold your ABN and your bank account details. We use them only to pay your
referral rewards. Our privacy policy explains how we handle personal information, how long we keep it,
and how to ask us about it.

You must not give us personal information about anyone else in connection with this program. As
clause 2 says, there is nowhere to do it, and we will not accept it.

### 11. How you may talk about us

When you share your code or link, please describe our products, prices and service accurately. Don't
say things about AMJ Trade Direct that aren't true, don't use our logo or branding as though you were
us or acting for us, and don't send unsolicited bulk messages.

### 12. You are not our agent

Taking part in the program does not make you our agent, partner, employee or representative. You
cannot enter into anything on our behalf or make promises that bind us.

### 13. Tax

> **FLAGGED FOR ACCOUNTANT REVIEW.** Under GST Act s 9-75 a stated amount is GST-inclusive unless the
> agreement says otherwise, which means a GST-registered referrer nets less than an unregistered one
> for the same referral. The position below is the default one and it must be confirmed — as must
> whether an RCTI arrangement is used, and whether the written agreement it requires sits here or in
> the RCTI itself. See research §1.4, §2.3 and §3.

The amount we tell you is the amount that reaches your bank account. **It includes any GST payable on
it.** If you are registered for GST, that means your GST is included in what we pay, not added to it.

Your own tax is your responsibility. We don't give tax advice — talk to your accountant about how
referral income should be treated in your circumstances.

You need an ABN on your account to take part. This is why.

### 14. If you owe us money

If you owe us money on your trading account, we may apply a referral reward against what you owe
instead of transferring it, and we will tell you when we do. We may pause your participation while
your account is overdue.

### 15. Which law applies

These terms are governed by the law of Victoria, Australia, and the courts of Victoria have
jurisdiction.

### 16. Where these terms live

These terms are published at this page and linked from the referral program page. The current version
applies to referrals recorded from the date shown below.

Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be
limited by agreement.

## Notes for whoever reviews this

**Clause-list coverage** (research §6): 1→§1, 2→§2, 3→§3, 4→§4, 5→§6, 6→§13, 7→§7, 8→§7, 9→§8, 10→§9,
11→§10, 12→§11, 13→§12, 14→§14, 15→§15, 16→§16. Clause 5's "minimum payout balance" is absent because
that feature was removed. The two-sided discount is §5, which the research's list predates.

**Three things drafted deliberately against the obvious wording:**

1. **Clause 1 leads with "you do not need to have bought anything."** ACL s 49 makes referral selling a
   strict-liability offence, and the thing that keeps this program outside it is that the reward is not
   an inducement to purchase. That fact has to be prominent, not buried.
2. **Clause 8 gives a review path rather than reserving discretion.** "We may withhold rewards where we
   suspect abuse" would be simpler and is a UCT exposure under s 25(h). Objective criteria plus notice
   plus a chance to respond is the defensible version.
3. **Clause 7 is narrow and forward-only**, and states the three things that survive a change. The
   research is clear this is the highest-risk clause under s 24 and the one the ACCC's loyalty-scheme
   work is most pointed about.

**What is not here and may need to be:** an RCTI agreement (§2.3 says it can be embedded in the RCTI
itself, which may be simpler); a stated notice period in clause 7; and confirmation of the entity name
and ABN this trades under.
