## What the owner asked the UX stage for

> "Run UX as well, as part of specs: layout of fields in the edit panel
> (right slideout, modal)."

So the deliverable is **the arrangement of fields inside the panel** — not the
container, which is settled, and not new visual language.

## Settled, not yours to revisit

- **Container:** `src/ops2/chrome/SidePanel.tsx`, unmodified. Right-hand
  slide-out at the desk, bottom sheet on the phone, `min(88vw, 520px)`. Grill
  D8. It is NOT the mock's full-page "Re-price" frame with a back arrow.
- **The door:** the line page's Price panel becomes openable through
  `OpenablePanel` — the same component and the same centred chevron the Why
  panel uses, unmodified. Owner: *"I meant the panel component with chevron,
  the same as Why this product uses."*
- **No clear action.** Entry is one-way (spec criterion 14, owner 2026-08-31).
  The panel must offer no way to remove a stored manufacturer price.
- **ex-GST throughout.** The basis switch declares what the TYPED figure
  includes and converts on storage; it is not a display toggle. Grill D6.
- **Quantity is 1.** No per-unit vs per-line affordance, no quantity shown
  beside the field.
- No new colour, border or card state — ops2's existing tokens only.

## What the mock's ManufacturerPricePage carries, as content

Reproduce the substance, rearranged for a 520px panel rather than a page:

1. A lede naming the current list price and what replaces it.
2. The entry-basis control (ex GST / inc GST) — labelled as being about the
   figure being typed.
3. The manufacturer's price field, with its "what they quoted on the call"
   helper.
4. The uplift field, defaulting to 30, helper naming 30 as standard and this
   change as being for this line.
5. The three-row arithmetic, appearing only once the entry is valid: their
   price → `+ N% uplift` → line price.
6. A footer read-back: what this line becomes with the old figure struck
   through, and what the quote total becomes.
7. The note that confirming does not change the project's "waiting on the
   manufacturer" state.

## The questions the layout has to answer

- Where the arithmetic sits relative to the two inputs, given a phone sheet has
  far less height than the mock's page.
- Whether the read-back is pinned to the panel's footer beside the confirm
  action, or scrolls with the content.
- What the panel shows before the entry is valid, so the space does not jump
  when the arithmetic appears.
- How the disabled confirm action reads before anything is typed.

Deliver `docs/mocks/manufacturer-price-uplift.html` — a single self-contained
file, ops2's tokens, showing the panel at desk width and at phone width.
