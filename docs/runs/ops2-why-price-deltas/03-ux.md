# 03 — UX: price deltas on the "Why this product?" ladder

Mock (the approval gate): `docs/mocks/ops2-why-price-deltas.html`
Inputs: `01-spec.md` (AC1–AC20), `02-design.md` (the hand-off index).

This document is the contract for what gets built once the owner approves the
mock. Where it says "unchanged", the developer changes nothing there.

---

## 1. Scope of the visual change

One surface: the candidate ladder inside `WhyDetail` — the "What else was
considered" section. Everything else on the detail (the requirement block, the
platform-pick comparison cards, the closing line) renders exactly as it does
today.

`WhyPanel` — the three-line summary card behind the `OpenablePanel` door — is
**not touched**. See §7 for why, and for the one-line change if the owner
overrides that.

---

## 2. The row, restructured (D5 / AC19)

### Today

```
AMJ67T Awning                          Uw 3.41 · SHGC 0.39     ← .wd__row-top (flex, space-between)
met the caps                                                   ← .wd__row-verdict
```

Two facts on line one, one beneath. A third fact can only join line one, and at
375px the product name is what would give way — which fails AC19 directly.

### After

```
AMJ67T Awning                                            +$65
Uw 3.41 · SHGC 0.39 · met the caps
```

A two-column grid:

| | Column 1 | Column 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Row 1 | product name (+ chosen mark) | the amount, right-aligned |
| Row 2 | evidence: `figures · verdict` | — |

Why this arrangement and not another:

- **The name leads, alone, and is free to wrap.** It is the row's identity; it
  is the one string whose length is unbounded (a 36-character product name is
  real — see the mock, frame D2) and it must never truncate.
- **Figures and verdict merge into one clause.** They are one statement about
  one candidate: *Uw 3.41 · SHGC 0.39 · met the caps*. Merging them is what
  frees line 1 for the amount without adding a third line. Row height is
  unchanged from today at desk width.
- **The amount gets a column of its own.** Five amounts that shift left and
  right with the length of the product name beside them get read five times
  instead of once. A fixed right edge is what makes the ladder answer "was it
  worth it?" in a single downward scan.

The amount is baseline-aligned with the name, not centred on the row.

## 3. The amount cell — every state

| Cell | When | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `+$65` | runner-up dearer than the chosen product (`deltaToSelected > 0`) | ink, semibold, tabular, explicit `+` |
| `-$200` | runner-up cheaper (`deltaToSelected < 0`) | identical treatment |
| `$0` | runner-up recorded at the same total (`deltaToSelected === 0`) | identical treatment, unsigned |
| `$---` | `deltaToSelected` is `null`, **or** the record predates price on the candidate contract | muted colour, regular weight |
| *(nothing rendered)* | the chosen row | no element, no placeholder, no whitespace reserve |

Rules that follow from the table:

- **Never coloured.** No green for cheaper, no red for dearer, no chip, no
  arrow glyph. Figures are not coloured anywhere in this console, and a green
  `-$50` beside a product that *failed its caps* would turn an explanation into
  a recommendation — which R28 forbids. The sign carries the direction and
  nothing else needs to.
- **`$0` and `$---` must be tellable apart at a glance.** `$0` is a recorded
  fact (two products priced the same) and reads as ink/semibold like any other
  amount. `$---` is the absence of a fact and reads muted/regular. They are
  never the same weight, and `$---` is **not** italic — `.ops2-absent`'s italic
  treatment applied to a run of dashes reads as damage rather than as absence.
- **Never `$0` for the uncalculable case** (AC5/D4), never a blank cell, never
  a hidden row.
- **The chosen row carries no amount at all** (AC4). It is the baseline every
  other amount is measured from; `+$0` there would claim a direction that does
  not exist. The empty cell is made legible by copy, not by a placeholder — see
  §4.
- **No date stamp, no "as at selection", no staleness hedge**, on any row or
  near the ladder (AC8).
- **Nothing is pressable** (AC20/R28). No handler, no chevron, no hover state,
  no focus stop, no `<button>`. The only interactive element on the detail
  remains back.

### The money face

The amount uses this console's money treatment — **body face, semibold,
tabular** — matching `.rl-money` (`record.css:336`). It deliberately does
**not** use `.wd__fig`'s display face, which on this screen means "a
performance figure". A dollar amount must never read as a Uw value.

## 4. Copy

### The ladder note — both branches change

Today the note under the ladder has two forms. Both gain the clause that names
what the amounts are measured from. This is the sentence that makes the chosen
row's empty cell legible; without it, a reader can plausibly conclude the value
failed to render.

**Five rows (chosen + four runners-up)** — replaces the current string, which
ends "No price, nothing to price, and nothing here changes the line.":

> The chosen product and the next four by rank. Each amount is that product's
> difference from the chosen one, and nothing here changes the line.

**Fewer than five rows** — the existing sentence with the clause appended:

> {count} candidate{s} {was|were} recorded for this opening — the list is what
> exists, with nothing padded and no remainder counted. Each amount is that
> product's difference from the chosen one.

> **Deviation from `02-design.md`:** the design rewrote only the five-row
> branch. Without the second rewrite a three-row ladder shows amounts that
> nothing on the screen has explained. Both branches change.

### Everything else — unchanged

`DETAIL.ladder` ("What else was considered"), `chosenRowMark` (`· chosen` /
`· the platform's pick`), `verdictWord`, `figuresText`, `DETAIL.closing` are all
untouched.

### Banned vocabulary (AC7)

No copy added by this feature may contain: `gst`, `tax`, `inc`, `ex`, `incl`,
`excl`, `inclusive`, `exclusive`, or `AUD`. That includes the aria-labels in
§5 — they are rendered text as far as the test is concerned. The strings above
and in §5 are already clean; keep them that way.

## 5. Screen readers (accessibility addition)

A bare `+` glyph is the one part of this design that does not survive being read
aloud: most screen readers announce `+$65` as "sixty-five dollars", losing the
direction entirely — and direction is the whole point of the figure.

Each amount therefore carries a spoken label the eye never sees. The visible
string is unchanged.

| Visible | Spoken |
|---|---|
| `+$65` | `$65 dearer than the chosen product` |
| `-$200` | `$200 cheaper than the chosen product` |
| `$0` | `the same price as the chosen product` |
| `$---` | `no price difference was recorded` |

Implementation: a `deltaLabel(delta: number \| null, chosen: boolean): string \| null`
companion beside `deltaText` in `whyCopy.ts` (same module, same reason — the
node test that scans copy for banned vocabulary sees it). Applied as
`aria-label` on the same `<span>` that carries the visible text.

> **Deviation from `02-design.md`:** the design named `deltaText` only. This
> adds one pure function beside it. Accessibility is on ponytail's
> "never be lazy about" list, and the cost is four strings.

## 6. Files and the exact changes

### `src/ops2/projects/whyCopy.ts`

1. `deltaText(delta, chosen)` — as designed, with one correction: the returned
   string must carry the `$`. The design's snippet builds
   `` `${Math.abs(r).toLocaleString("en-AU")}` `` and returns `+65`, but AC2
   requires `+$100` and AC5 requires `$---`. Correct form:

   ```ts
   export function deltaText(delta: number | null, chosen: boolean): string | null {
     if (chosen) return null;
     if (delta == null) return "$---";
     const r = Math.round(delta);
     const figure = `$${Math.abs(r).toLocaleString("en-AU")}`;
     return r === 0 ? figure : `${r < 0 ? "-" : "+"}${figure}`;
   }
   ```

   Whole dollars, `en-AU` grouping (`+$1,840`). `chosen` returning `null` is
   what produces "no element" rather than an empty one.
2. `deltaLabel(delta, chosen)` — new, per §5. Returns `null` when `chosen`.
3. `ladderNote` — both branches gain the clause, per §4.

No formatting in JSX. Every string on this screen comes from this module.

### `src/ops2/projects/WhyDetail.tsx`

Row markup becomes:

```jsx
<li className={i === 0 ? "wd__row wd__row--chosen" : "wd__row"} data-testid="why-ladder-row">
  <span className="wd__row-name">
    {candidateName(c)}
    {i === 0 && <span className="wd__row-mark"> {chosenRowMark(dto.selectionChanged)}</span>}
  </span>
  <span className="wd__row-meta">
    <span className="wd__row-figs">{candidateFigures(c)}</span>
    <span className="wd__row-verdict">{verdictWord(c, dto.requirement, dto.tolerance)}</span>
  </span>
  {delta !== null && (
    <span
      className={delta === "$---" ? "wd__row-delta wd__row-delta--absent" : "wd__row-delta"}
      data-testid="why-row-delta"
      aria-label={deltaLabel(...)}
    >
      {delta}
    </span>
  )}
</li>
```

Changes from today: `.wd__row-top` disappears (the grid replaces it),
`.wd__row-figs` and `.wd__row-verdict` move inside a new `.wd__row-meta`
wrapper, and the delta span is appended. The `data-testid="why-row-delta"` seam
is the design's, unchanged. `.wd__row` / `.wd__row--chosen` /
`data-testid="why-ladder-row"` keep their names, so existing tests that select
rows keep working.

The `--absent` class is chosen from the rendered string rather than re-deriving
the null test — one source of truth for "this is not an amount".

### `src/ops2/styles/line.css`

Replace the `.wd__row*` block with the rules in the mock's stylesheet (search
`THE LADDER`). Summary:

- `.wd__row` — becomes `display:grid; grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) auto;
  column-gap:var(--theme-spacing-md); padding-block:var(--theme-spacing-xs)`
  (was `2xs`; the second column needs the air). `row-gap`, `border-top`,
  `font-size` unchanged.
- `.wd__row-top` — **deleted**.
- `.wd__row-name` — `grid-column:1`, `overflow-wrap:anywhere`. Colour and the
  `--chosen` medium weight unchanged.
- `.wd__row-meta` — new. `grid-column:1; display:flex; flex-wrap:wrap;
  gap:0 var(--theme-spacing-sm); font-size:var(--theme-font-size-xs)`.
- `.wd__row-figs` — keeps its display face and tabular numerals; loses only its
  `flex:none` (it is no longer a flex child of `.wd__row-top`).
- `.wd__row-verdict` — gains `::before{content:"· "}` so the evidence reads as
  one clause. Colour and size unchanged.
- `.wd__row-delta` — new, per §3.
- `.wd__row-delta--absent` — new, per §3.

No new tokens, no new colours, no radius above `--theme-radius-sm`. **No
`prefers-color-scheme` block** — ops2 ships light only.

## 7. `WhyPanel` — deliberately unchanged

The summary card's whole budget is three lines (R6/D20) and each answers
*what*: what it had to meet, what this one is, why it was chosen. The amounts
answer *why the others lost*, which is a five-row comparison and belongs with
the five rows. A single amount on the card would have nothing visible to be a
difference from.

`01-spec.md` §4 flags this as the owner's to veto. If the owner wants it on the
card, it is one added line and AC1 extends there; say so at mock approval.

## 8. States, end to end

| State | What renders |
|---|---|
| **Populated, machine-recommended** | Five rows, amounts on four. Mock frame B1. |
| **Populated, human-changed line** | Comparison cards unchanged and money-free; ladder amounts still measured from the *platform's pick* (the chosen row), whose mark reads `· the platform's pick`. A later human change does not re-base the amounts. Mock frame B2. |
| **Short ladder (<5)** | Fewer rows, short-form note with the appended clause. Mock frame B2. |
| **Mixed** | `+`, `-`, `$0` and `$---` coexist in one ladder without any of them being mistakable for another. Mock frame C1. |
| **Pre-price record** (AC6) | Every runner-up reads `$---`; the rest of the detail renders exactly as today. No banner, no "price unavailable for older runs", no empty panel, no error. Mock frame C2. |
| **Long content** | 36-character product name wraps in column 1; the amount column does not move. Split candidates keep `2 units` where their figures go and still carry an amount. Mock frames C1, D2. |
| **Loading / error** | Unchanged. This feature adds no fetch and no request — the deltas arrive in the DTO the detail already loads (R3: snapshot surface, no recompute at display time). The existing loading and error paths of the detail are untouched. |
| **Phone, 375px** | Detail is full-screen (`phoneForm="screen"`), unchanged. The two-column row holds: the evidence clause wraps to a second line where needed, the name does not truncate, the amount stays in its column. Mock frames D1, D2. |

# CHANGES REQUESTED — owner, 2026-09-04, at the mock gate

Two rejections and one confirmation.

## 1. REJECTED — one long panel is not an information architecture

> *"i don't like that information is organised into single panel - its difficult
> to read. organise better. panels. lists (of panels), maybe? Don't give me a
> lazy solution."*

The detail is currently one scroll of stacked blocks separated by headings. A
heading is not a boundary: at a glance it reads as one undifferentiated column,
and the reader has to parse prose to find where one fact ends and the next
begins. Adding a price column made it worse, because there is now a second
column of figures competing for the same eye line.

**Re-architect it as CARDS, the way the rest of ops2 already works.** The
console's own vocabulary already has the pieces and they are not being used
here: `OpenablePanel` is the card (`CONTEXT.md` §Openable panel — and since the
delivery feature its `title` is optional, so a card whose contents are
self-labelling needs no heading), and `RowList` is the one pressable row and its
list container. The record page, the line page and the totals card are all
already built from them. **This surface is the outlier, and that is the defect.**

Direction, not a layout to copy verbatim — the UX stage owns the specifics and
must justify what it chooses:

- **Each distinct fact becomes its own card**, with its own edge, so the eye can
  skip a whole card rather than reading to the end of a paragraph to discover it
  was not the card it wanted. Candidates for cards: the requirement and where it
  came from · what is unusual about this line, when anything is · the comparison
  block when a person moved the pick (B2) · the ladder.
- **The ladder is a LIST OF ROWS INSIDE ONE CARD**, not five loose blocks and
  not five cards. Five cards would give five borders to a set that is one idea;
  the rows belong to each other and their alignment is the whole point of the
  two-column grid this pass introduced. Keep that grid.
- **Nothing becomes pressable.** R28 still holds — the only interactive element
  on this surface is back. `OpenablePanel` takes openability as the PRESENCE of
  `open`; these cards simply do not pass one. A card is a container here, not a
  door.
- **No new CSS vocabulary where the console already has one.** If a rule already
  exists on `.lp-panel` or `RowList`, reuse it. A `wd__`-prefixed
  reimplementation of a card is the failure this ruling is about.

The owner asked for panels and lists of panels and said explicitly not to give
him a lazy solution. A pass that only adds spacing, rules or heading weight has
not done the work.

## 2. REJECTED — the tablet presentation is not our slide-out

> *"on ipad there's a modal centered popup - that is NOT how our default sideout
> panel works (REUSE COMPONENTS!!!)"*

**This is a pre-existing chrome defect, not something this mock introduced**, and
it was identified earlier and left unfixed. `WhyDetail` renders through
`SidePanel` with `phoneForm="screen"` (`WhyDetail.tsx:46`). In
`src/ops2/styles/projects.css` there are sizing rules for
`ion-modal.pq-sheet--side` and **none whatsoever** for the plain `.pq-sheet` the
`screen` form uses — verified: zero matching rules. So that form falls through to
Ionic's stock `IonModal`, which is full-bleed only on a genuinely narrow phone
and a **centred inset card everywhere between it and the `--cp-shell-rail`
change point at 1024px**. That band is the iPad.

The component's own header calls `screen` *"full screen with no breakpoints,
which is what R26 asked for and what an `IonModal` does when given neither"* —
true on a phone, false on a tablet. The documentation and the behaviour disagree,
and the behaviour is wrong.

**Fix it in `SidePanel`'s stylesheet, not in this feature's CSS**, so every
caller of the `screen` form is fixed at once and nothing here reimplements a
panel. The `screen` form must be genuinely full-bleed at every width below the
change point: no inset, no centring, no rounded card floating on a backdrop.
Above the change point the surface is the desk's right-hand slide-out, unchanged.

Do not "fix" this by switching `WhyDetail` to `phoneForm="side"` — that changes
what the surface IS on a phone, which R26 settled, and leaves the defect in place
for the next caller.

## 3. CONFIRMED — B2 is not an invention

> *"B2 - do we have a state like that or is in a new invention?"*

It is **R11**, shipped: *"When a human has changed the make-up, show both. The
original platform recommendation stays, unchanged, and a comparison of the
human's selection against the requirement is added beside it."* The DTO carries
`current` alongside the recommendation and `rationale.ts` has a `pickMoved`
predicate. The mock draws an existing state; the only thing this feature adds to
it is the price column. No change required — recorded so the question is not
asked twice.
