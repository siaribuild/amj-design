The estimator page, built on your exploration’s approach

Your document contributes two ideas. One of them solves the problem I said was the cost of containing a child. Both are adopted below and re-expressed in the project’s real tokens, type scale and column tracks — the exploration used a different palette (#fa5252, #40c057) and no columns, because it was a phone sketch.

What the exploration gets right

  • The escaped stripe. .status-stripe { left: -26px } on a child inside a box that is itself margin-left: 26px. The child is contained, but its state marker is pulled back out to the record’s x=0. This is the answer to the objection I raised against B and C — that containing a child moves its stripe off the scan line. It does not have to. I did not think of it.
  • The child group as one contained box with a left spine — border-left plus a right margin, no left radius. It reads as a bounded sub-object rather than a band, which is the “different context view” you have been describing since the start.
  • Mobile first. The whole sketch is 375px. Every prior round of this discussion, mine included, designed at desktop and asked what happens to the phone afterwards.

What I have changed, and why

  • No green “OK” stripe on clean children. The sketch marks healthy rows green and neutral rows grey. This route’s rule is that a row says nothing at rest — a column of green under a clean parent is the per-line noise the whole design removes. Clean rows get no stripe; the eye then reads the left edge as exceptions only.
  • Muted state colours. --warning #8a6a2a and --info #4C6A88, not red-500/amber-500. The palette is warm and low-chroma throughout; a pure red would be the loudest thing on the page by a wide margin.
  • Two states, not one. The sketch paints everything red. A coverage fault (“units don’t sum to the opening”) is ours to confirm and reads --info; something the customer must supply reads --warning. Case 4 below shows both in one record.
  • Never overflow-hidden on the child group. The sketch has it on case 2/3 — which would clip the escaped stripe it just invented.
  • Columns. A phone has none, so the sketch is silent. On desktop the child box is inset on both sides, so track 1 absorbs the left inset and the LAST track absorbs the right one, or Actions drifts. Measured below.
