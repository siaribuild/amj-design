The shifted child card

Three readings of the thing I kept failing to draw: a child that is its own card, stepped in from its parent — not a row indented inside the parent's card. Each is shown with a unit expanded, because that is the case that motivates the shift.

Column alignment is preserved deliberately: a child card's FIRST track shrinks by exactly the shift, so Status, Product, Size and Price stay on the parent's and the header's x. That is the real cost of the idea — it needs a second track list — and it is measured at the bottom of each section.
