ops2 Attention — AI parse monitoring

The /attention route stops being a placeholder and becomes the page that answers two questions in one glance: is the AI budget about to run out, and is parsing healthy. Every figure comes from one KV snapshot written by the existing */10 cron. The page makes no Cloudflare call and holds no threshold of its own. Mock for approval; not production code.

Four tiles in two named pairs, then the seven-day chart. The pairs are the two questions; the chart is the same numbers spread over days, drawn in the same two hues as the count tiles so the card and its bars read as one fact. Red is the only alarm in v1: a low balance, or a cap near its ceiling. It is stated once, on the card that carries the fault, with a 1 on the bell (desk) or the Attention tab (phone). Errored parses are not an alarm and never paint the page red.

Decisions this mock is asking you to approve

1 · Two named pairs, not five loose cards. "AI budget" and "Parsing · last 7 days" are the two questions; the tiles sit under the question they answer.
2 · The freshness line lives in the band, once (OpsPage's existing identity slot), and only a stale snapshot stamps every card individually.
3 · Cap outstanding is labelled "Cap remaining" and shows the percentage used beside the money, because the ceiling is a percentage.
4 · Errored parses are never painted as an alarm. Only the budget goes red, because only the budget raises the bubble.
5 · Chart numerals show at the desk, not on the phone.
6 · No dark mode — ops2 has no dark theme today, and this page does not invent one.
