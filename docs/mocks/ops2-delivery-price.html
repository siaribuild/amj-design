Two facts, two places. The price is settled from the totals card: the whole card is the door — one chevron centred against the card, the three rows stay plain text (D17). Nothing is drawn below Project total and there is no separate Delivery card (D9). The address is a five-field postal address owned by the project, and it is the first real content on the Project tab (D10). Both open the same right-hand slide-out at every width. Throughout: no tax word anywhere (D11), never two delivery figures on screen (D12), a locked project shows the figures as plain text with no reason line (D13), and unpriced is the ordinary state, not an error (D8).
