Price deltas on the “Why this product?” ladder

The estimator arrives at this screen with one question — was it worth it? — and today the screen cannot answer it. Two products met the caps; only one was picked; the ladder shows both and says nothing about why the other lost. The figure that closes that sentence is already on disk. This adds it, and only it — and re-lays the screen it lands on, because a third fact could not be read on the screen as it stands.

Revision 2 — what changed after your review of 2026-09-04
  • “Difficult to read. Organise better. Panels. Lists (of panels).” The detail is no longer one scroll of headed blocks. It is now the line page’s own shape: lp-body on the page ground, carrying lp-panel cards — the same card the specification, review and units panels are. The ladder is one card containing a list of rows, not five loose blocks and not five cards. See A.
  • Nothing is re-implemented. Three wd__ rules are deleted because the console already had them: .wd__blk-h was a byte-identical copy of .lp-panel__title, .wd__kv of .lp-panel__lines, and the block wrapper of .lp-panel itself. The ladder rows carry ops2-row, so the hairline between them is rows.css’s existing rule rather than a fourth copy of it. Net CSS after this feature is smaller than before it.
  • “on ipad there’s a modal centered popup - that is NOT how our default sideout panel works (REUSE COMPONENTS!!!)” Correct, and it is a defect in SidePanel that predates this feature. Fixed in the shared component, so every caller of the full-screen form is fixed at once. See D.
  • “B2 — do we have a state like that?” Shipped, R11: when a person has changed the make-up, the platform’s recommendation stays and the line’s own configuration is shown beside it. Not an invention.
  • Still nothing pressable. Cards here are containers, not doors — OpenablePanel takes openability as the presence of open, and these pass none. The only control on the screen is still back.
What this mock holds itself to
  • One number per candidate. No GST wording, no basis switch, no second figure, no total. An amount, or nothing.
  • The money is an explanation, not a comparison. Nothing coloured green or red, nothing that invites a switch.
  • The chosen row carries no amount. It is the baseline the others are measured from — “+$0” would claim a direction that does not exist.
  • An uncalculable delta reads $--- — never $0, never a blank cell, never a hidden row.
  • Membership does not change. The chosen product and the next four by rank, exactly as today.
  • No date stamp, no “as at selection”, no staleness hedge anywhere near the amount.

D · Tablet, 834px — the slide-out, fixed

Your second rejection, and it is a real defect that predates this feature. The component own comment claims “full screen with no breakpoints”; the behaviour disagrees, and the behaviour is wrong. The fix belongs in the SidePanel stylesheet, so every caller of the full-screen form is fixed at once and this feature re-implements no panel.
