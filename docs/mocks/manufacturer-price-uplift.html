Manufacturer's price and uplift

The estimator has a figure from the manufacturer, usually read to them on a call. They enter it, the platform applies the uplift, and the working is shown before anything is committed. Container is SidePanel, unmodified; the door is the Price panel through OpenablePanel, unmodified. A price is a price: the same manufacturer pricelist as every other line, priced ad-hoc for this project — so no new state wording, no restated GST basis, and nothing on the line page reads differently afterwards.
